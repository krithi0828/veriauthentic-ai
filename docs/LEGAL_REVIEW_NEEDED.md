# Legal Review Needed Notes ⚖️

The Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Decision-Support Disclaimers rendered in the web application interface are provided as sensible production-ready UI templates. Before commercial deployment, counsel should review and finalize the following terms:

1. **Risk Score Disclaimer Clause**: Ensure explicit limitation of liability regarding marketplace purchasing decisions based on model risk scores.
2. **Trademark & Brand Intellectual Property**: Ensure fair-use brand reference statements for benchmark dataset names (e.g. Nike, Rolex, Sony).
3. **Data Protection & GDPR Compliance**: Confirm cookie consent and session storage practices.
